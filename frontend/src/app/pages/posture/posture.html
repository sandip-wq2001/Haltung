Posture instrument (author-side)

Posture threshold finding

Records all seven posture metrics: head tilt, shoulder tilt, their difference, IPD, and the three IPD-normalized ratios. The app speaks each pose, waits while you get into it, then records only the held pose. Run the same script from each camera position and keep your posture the same — any change in the numbers is then caused by the camera, not by you.
